# 03 — T5

## Architecture

**Encoder-decoder** Transformer with a unified text-to-text framework.

## Text-to-Text Framework

Every NLP task is cast as text generation:

| Task | Input | Output |
|------|-------|--------|
| Translation | translate English to German: The cat... | Die Katze... |
| Summarization | summarize: Long article... | Short summary |
| Classification | classify sentiment: Great movie! | positive |
| QA | question: What is 2+2? context: Math... | 4 |

## Pre-training

**Objective**: Span corruption (denoising)
```
Input:  "Thank you <X> me to your party <Y> week."
Target: "<X> for inviting <Y> last"
```

Replace consecutive spans with sentinel tokens. Predict the dropped spans.

## Architecture Details

| Variant | d_model | Layers | Heads | d_ff | Params |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|------|--------|
| T5-Small | 512 | 6 | 8 | 2048 | 60M |
| T5-Base | 768 | 12 | 12 | 3072 | 220M |
| T5-Large | 1024 | 24 | 16 | 4096 | 770M |
| T5-11B | 1024 | 24 | 128 | 65536 | 11B |

## Key Takeaway

T5 demonstrated that a single encoder-decoder architecture with a unified text-to-text interface can handle diverse NLP tasks. The span corruption objective is effective for pre-training.
